About Us

Long-term owners of businesses that serve their communities

KRS Business Group is a privately held company that owns and operates a portfolio of businesses. We bring each of them the structure of a larger organization — centralized compliance, licensing, insurance, and financial oversight — while letting every location stay close to the customers it serves.

Our focus is simple: keep every business well run, fully compliant, and positioned to grow for the long term.
